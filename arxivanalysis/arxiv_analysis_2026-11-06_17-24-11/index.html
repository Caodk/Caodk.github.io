中文标题: 基于质量守恒混合虚拟元的多边形与切割网格变饱和流动

摘要翻译: 提出了一种混合虚拟元格式（HVEM），用于由 Richards 方程控制的变饱和土壤中的瞬态流动。节点水头是唯一的全局自由度，并在每个多边形单元中假定 Darcy 通量并将其凝聚。该通量将无散度部分与一个散度为线性的特定场相结合，后者吸收储水项和源项，从而每个单元的水量平衡在每个迭代步和任意时间步下恒等成立，并在收敛时整个区域的水量平衡成立。为保证无需稳定化参数即可满秩，无散度场采用按其旋转下的行为排序的调和多项式，从而给出通量参数数目的闭式规则；该规则在正多边形上推导，并在畸变多边形和带悬挂节点的单元上数值验证。在每个单元中针对 Kirchhoff 势写出 Darcy 定律，这消除了扩散项中的导水率，同时水头在材料界面处保持连续。导水率的 Kirchhoff 平均使静水平衡和重力排水在任何网格上都是精确的。储水项按节点集中，其权重由假定通量提供。所有单元算子都化为边界积分，因此非线性迭代只需节点值。该格式在闭式解、已发表基准以及均质与分层边坡上的降雨入渗问题上进行评估，其中独立解提供了进一步校验。在多头和局部加密网格上，水头以二阶收敛，在重力占优时亦然，并在每个测试中闭合水量收支。在分层边坡上，沿地表条带加密的切割网格以约 40% 的节点数达到细一倍的均匀网格的精度。其结构与用于弹塑性的 HVEM 共享，适用于边坡耦合分析。

发布日期: 2026-11-05

arXiv ID: 2611.08261v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08261v1

摘要: The Characteristic Mapping (CM) method is a transport scheme which discretizes the Lagrangian flow map as a composition of spatial interpolants. This adaptive approximation technique has demonstrated novel resolution properties and the ability to efficiently simulate the fine scales of inviscid turbulent flows. In this work, we construct an extension of this approach to the solution of dissipative problems, including the Navier-Stokes equations. The extension lifts a general scalar evolution equation to a path in a group of diffeomorphisms, where the semigroup approach of the CM method can be exploited. We give an interpretation of this lift through the geometric framework of optimal transport. We then introduce a semidirect product evolution technique to perform the time stepping of the map components. The map is evolved based on the semi-Lagrangian CM approach, and its log-Jacobian is evolved explicitly by an exponential integrator. The numerical scheme is demonstrated to be high-order accurate and is tested on a range of canonical nonlinear dissipative equations. By retaining the resolution properties of the CM method, the approach shows a promising ability to simulate turbulent fluid flows across all Reynolds numbers.

摘要: We prove that Schumaker’s lower bound gives the dimension of $S_{3r+1}^r(Δ)$ for every nondegenerate triangulation $Δ$ of a polygonal disk in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and every integer $r\ge3$. No synthetic field has carried both, because the frame that gives the first destroys the field the second requires. The key is a dichotomy: deforming a spectrally localised pattern smears it across scales, whereas pushing its generating centres forward and re-convolving leaves the spectral envelope exactly invariant — so transporting the eddy centres preserves both cascade and frame. In the field the finite-size Lyapunov exponent obeys $ζ(R)=h(R)-1$ threshold by threshold within the scaling range, with a sweeping deficit $Δ_{\rm sw}=+0.038\pm0.019$, consistent with zero, against $+0.091\pm0.019$ for the frozen field on the same data, a gap that doubles at Navier–Stokes intermittency. Companion papers prove that this family shows Richardson dispersion and a dissipative anomaly (Fefferman and Mayboroda 2026), and that a scalar advected by this flow obeys Yaglom’s relation (De Lellis 2026), at small Kubo number. Trajectories forget their starting point as the smallest eddy is removed: the median separation of pairs released from one point with independent Brownian kicks varies as $κ_{p}^{0.0333\pm0.0038}$ over 22 decades of particle diffusivity, against $κ_{p}^{1/2}$ for a smooth control. 14. 一种用于 6D Vlasov 模拟的基于物理的自适应低秩方法#

A physics-based adaptive low-rank method for 6D Vlasov simulations

作者: Fabio Cassini, Lukas Einkemmer

类别: math.NA, physics.plasm-ph

发布日期: 2026-11-04

arXiv ID: 2611.07107v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07107v1

摘要: The numerical simulation of kinetic plasma models in six-dimensional phase space poses significant computational and memory challenges, particularly when considering modern high-performance computing architectures. In this work, we propose an adaptive dynamical low-rank approach for the efficient solution of the 6D Vlasov–Poisson system. Our strategy is an extension of the projector-splitting dynamical low-rank integrator. The algorithm changes the rank based on physical observables (e.g., the electric field and energy) as opposed to controlling the error in the distribution function. Our approach is memory efficient in the sense that only rank $r$ objects are required to implement the scheme. This makes the algorithm suitable for performing high-dimensional computations on graphic processing units. Comparisons with rank adaptive Basis-Update & Galerkin integrators exhibit both significant memory savings and reduction in run time. We show, by presenting a variety of 6D benchmark experiments implemented in the C++/CUDA low-rank framework Ensign, that our technique allows us to run simulations with a significantly smaller rank, while still preserving the physical fidelity. Thus, the approach substantially reduces memory footprint and computational cost and enables simulations that are prohibitively expensive for full-rank or less memory-efficient low-rank schemes.
