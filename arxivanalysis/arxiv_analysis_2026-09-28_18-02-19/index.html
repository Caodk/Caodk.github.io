摘要翻译#

中文标题: NEXT：物理信息神经谱指数时间差分架构

摘要翻译: 物理信息神经网络（PINNs）构建含时偏微分方程解的神经表示，自然地融合物理知识与观测数据，这使得它们非常适合正向与逆向 PDE 问题。然而，PINNs 已知会遭受谱偏差和缺乏因果性的问题。神经谱架构（NeuSA）是最近提出的 PINNs 替代方案，缓解了这两个问题，但它们的数值积分对于许多相关物理问题中出现的刚性微分方程会变得不稳定。本研究提出神经谱指数时间差分架构（NEXT），它将 NeuSA 中 PDE 解的谱表示与高阶指数积分器相结合。在该方法中，由 PDE 诱导的向量场的线性刚性部分通过矩阵指数被精确积分，而可能非线性的剩余部分则由神经网络建模。NEXT 的有效性通过在一组刚性 PDE 上的基准实验得到验证，在这些实验中，NEXT 稳定且准确，而 NeuSA 在数值上发散。还表明 NEXT 可应用于反问题，其中模型必须从稀疏数据中学习未知参数或边界条件。本工作使用的所有代码公开于：https://github.com/marcioh2m/next.git 。

摘要翻译: 本文针对气体动力学中的可压缩 Euler 方程，构造了一种新的半隐式 (SI)、双曲且热力学相容 (HTC) 格式。其中的挑战在于将熵不等式作为主要演化方程求解，而不是求解总能量守恒律；在我们的框架中，总能量守恒律是一个附加守恒律，需要由数值格式作为相容离散化的结果来满足。所提方法具有预测-校正特征，其中我们首先在不考虑熵产生的情况下求解等熵问题。在此过程中，熵以及动量通量中的非线性对流项被显式离散，而质量通量和压力项则被隐式处理。然后，将离散动量方程代入离散质量守恒方程，得到一个关于压力的弱非线性系统，可用嵌套 Newton 型算法高效求解。在此预测的基础上，加入熵中的非负产生项，从而保证数值格式满足单元熵不等式。为保证离散层面上的总能量全局守恒，引入了一种新的修正全局 Abgrall 型通量校正。所提出的数值方法允许时间步长仅受物质波速限制，而不受声速限制。这使得该格式特别适用于低 Mach 数区域的流动。给出一维和二维空间中的数值结果，以评估新格式的理论性质。

1. 皮质骨与松质骨电学性质的数值模拟：一个简化模型#

Numerical Simulation of Electrical Properties in Cortical and Trabecular Bone: A Simplified Model

作者: María José Cervantes, Catalina A. Cely-Ortíz, C. Manuel Carlevaro, Ramiro M. Irastorza

类别: physics.med-ph, math.NA

发布日期: 2026-09-25

arXiv ID: 2609.31584v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31584v1

摘要: The electrical properties of biological tissues depend on their composition and microstructure and determine their response to applied electric fields. In bone tissue, these properties are closely related to its composition, porosity, and microstructural organization. However, simplified models that link bone microstructure (trabecular and cortical) to its effective electrical properties while accounting for the electrical anisotropy of cortical bone remain scarce. In this work, a multiscale approach was developed, combining finite element simulations with Bruggeman-based homogenization models. Trabecular bone was modeled using microCT derived geometries, and its effective conductivity was analyzed as a function of BV/TV and free water content. Cortical bone was represented by a simplified three-dimensional network of Haversian canals (axial = z) and Volkmann canals (transverse), incorporating their directional organization through an anisotropic Bruggeman formulation. The models captured the conductivity range reported experimentally for trabecular bone (approximately 25-200 mS/m) and reproduced the anisotropic behavior observed in the cortical FEM simulations, with higher conductivity in the axial direction. In the generated cortical geometries, 65.5% of the total porosity was associated with axial Haversian canals. At the macroscale, the tibia model with lower trabecular and cortical BV/TV and reduced cortical thickness showed an approximately 38-39% reduction in impedance relative to the reference model. These results indicate that microstructural variations in bone tissue are reflected in its effective electrical properties and in the macroscopic impedance of the model, providing a physical and computational basis for future investigations into the sensitivity of electrical measurements to changes in bone microstructure.

Boundary Value Correction for the Boundary Element Method

作者: Erik Burman, Peter Hansbo, Mats G. Larson

类别: math.NA

发布日期: 2026-09-25

arXiv ID: 2609.31555v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31555v1

摘要: We develop a boundary element method for the Laplace equation with Dirichlet boundary conditions in a bounded three-dimensional domain. The method uses flat panels of maximum diameter (h) on an interior surrogate boundary and corrects the imposed boundary values by a first-order Taylor expansion along the panel normals. The correction uses the normal flux already present in the Calderón formulation and introduces no additional unknown field. Under uniform assumptions on the surrogate geometry and boundary operators, the penalised form is coercive when (γδ_h\le c_0<4), where (γ) is the penalty parameter and (δ_h) is the maximum normal offset. The error estimate separates trace approximation from a geometry term of size (γ^{1/2}δ_h^2|u|{H^3(Ω)}). With (γ\simeq h^{-1}), the sufficient condition (δ_h\lesssim h^{(k+1)/2}) yields an error of order (h^{k+1/2}) for (P_k/P{k-1}) Dirichlet/flux spaces under the stated regularity and uniform trace bounds. Thus an offset of order (h^2) supports the estimate through cubic Dirichlet approximation. We analyse two penalty variants and present numerical experiments on a sphere and an ellipsoid to examine convergence and sensitivity to geometry, penalty scaling and quadrature.

类别: math.NA

发布日期: 2026-09-25

arXiv ID: 2609.31443v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31443v1

摘要: Low-order refined (LOR) preconditioning replaces a high-order operator with a low-order discretisation on a refined nodal mesh. For tensor-product elements, the two operators are spectrally equivalent with bounds independent of the polynomial order $P$, but the construction does not extend directly to simplex and mixed-element discretisations. This work makes two contributions: it extends LOR preconditioning to simplex and mixed-element discretisations, including triangular, tetrahedral, and prismatic elements, and establishes a generalised Vandermonde transformation linking the modal and nodal LOR formulations, showing that the resulting preconditioned spectra and Krylov convergence are independent of the high-order basis. A single algebraic multigrid V-cycle per outer iteration gives the best balance of iteration count and cost.
