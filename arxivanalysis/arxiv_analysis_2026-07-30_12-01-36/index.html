摘要翻译#

中文标题: 超导伦敦方程的数值方法

摘要翻译: 本文为求解全空间 $\mathbb{R}^3$ 中第二类超导体的伦敦方程提出一种通用的离散化策略。为了计算磁场 $H_0$，我们将磁位势问题重新表述为传输问题，并通过非标准有限元-边界元耦合方法对其进行离散。该表述同时考虑了有界内部区域和无界外部区域，且无需引入人工截断。随后，我们计算矢量场 $B_0$，它源自超导样品中磁位势的 Helmholtz-Hodge 分解。该场进入等通量问题，后者确定在 Ginzburg–Landau 超导模型中涡旋成核首次在能量上有利的曲线。我们使用 Kikuchi 的混合形式重写 $B_0$ 的方程，在该形式中无散度约束被弱施加，并采用经典的 $H(\operatorname{curl})$-协调有限元离散方法对所得问题进行离散。我们通过收敛性测试验证了离散化策略，并以等通量问题的应用作为总结。对于恒定外加磁场下的球体，唯一的极大化元是与磁场方向对齐的直径。对于恒定外加磁场与长轴对齐的椭球，我们的计算提供了在充分拉长的雪茄状几何体中不同行为的数值证据：非轴向竞争者（让人联想到 U 形涡旋构型）获得了比长轴更大的等通量比。因此，长轴并非极大化元；任意非轴向极大化元通过旋转对称性生成一个连续的等价构型族，这意味着等通量问题中的非唯一性和退化旋转方向的存在。

发布日期: 2026-07-29

arXiv ID: 2607.27196v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27196v1

摘要: We present a novel approach to regression tasks using classification which is motivated by the mechanism used by fruitflies to sense their environment. Specifically, we formulate a general framework for learning nonlinear input-output relationships by replacing complex global surrogate models with a finite library of representative local patterns. Since scientific data often occupy limited and recurring regions of the input space, we generate predictions by measuring similarities between a query and stored patterns, then combining their associated responses through weighted reconstruction. This structure helps us reduce computational and memory demands while providing explicit control over the trade-off among accuracy, storage, and inference cost.

2. 熵守恒的分部求和离散对双曲守恒律光滑解的收敛性#

Convergence of entropy-conservative summation-by-parts discretizations to smooth solutions of hyperbolic conservation laws

作者: Hendrik Ranocha

类别: math.NA

发布日期: 2026-07-29

arXiv ID: 2607.27049v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27049v1

摘要: Although entropy-based summation-by-parts (SBP) discretizations of hyperbolic conservation laws are widely used for their robustness and stability properties, there are very few results on their convergence. We extend a recent convergence analysis of Worku, Del Rey Fernández, and Zingg (2026, DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2603.18369) in two ways. First, instead of allowing only hyperbolic conservation laws whose fluxes are homogeneous and have globally bounded second derivatives (a restriction essentially to linear or quadratic fluxes), we consider general hyperbolic systems with strictly convex entropy and source terms depending on time and space. Second, instead of requiring a special class of SBP operators, we consider a general framework of diagonal-norm SBP operators on curved meshes, including finite differences, continuous and discontinuous Galerkin methods. To enable a unified treatment of conservation laws, we restrict the analysis to periodic boundary conditions. Numerical results demonstrate that the predicted convergence rates are sharp in general, but can be improved for special cases such as discontinuous Galerkin methods with even polynomial degree and multi-block finite difference methods. An optimal analysis is expected to require more sophisticated arguments specialized to the class of methods instead of the general framework of SBP operators used in this work.

类别: math.NA, physics.geo-ph

发布日期: 2026-07-29

arXiv ID: 2607.26951v1

链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26951v1

摘要: Linear inverse problems are prevalent in geophysics, signal processing, image restoration, and medical imaging. Mathematically, they can be formulated as $Gm = d$. When $G$ is ill-conditioned or singular, the problem becomes ill-posed and requires regularization methods or generalized inverse methods for a stable solution. However, both types of methods encounter significant computational difficulties for large-scale linear inverse problems. In this paper, we establish a spectral-domain pseudo-inverse construction method for a class of linear inverse problems that can be diagonalized by unitary matrices. The core idea is to construct a stable pseudo-inverse operator directly in the transform domain, starting from the unitary diagonalization structure of the matrix. This construction is numerically equivalent to zeroth-order Tikhonov regularization and converges to the Moore–Penrose generalized inverse as $α\to 0^+$, but its methodological path differs from both. This paper reveals that the stable generalized inverse of a class of structured matrices can be directly constructed from their spectral decomposition, providing an efficient solution method for large-scale structured inverse problems. 链接: http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26863v1

摘要: Pivoted QR and pivoted LU decompositions are greedy algorithms used to compute low-rank approximations of matrices from selected columns, or selected rows and columns.
